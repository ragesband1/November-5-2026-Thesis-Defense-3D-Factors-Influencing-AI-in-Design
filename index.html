Domain Analysis (Mean Likert Ratings)

Most Used AI Tools

AI Output vs Original Artwork

Top Driver (Productivity)

Students prioritize speed & convenience for task accomplishment, drastically decreasing visual layout iteration time.

Idea Generation Booster

AI serves primarily as an ideation catalyst and moodboard companion rather than producing finalized final graphics.

Primary Ethical Concern

High concern exists regarding potential loss of personal artistic originality, dependency, and unresolved copyright issues.

Pedagogical Recommendation

Multimedia curricula should integrate clear ethical guidelines and transparent AI attribution standards for design projects.
